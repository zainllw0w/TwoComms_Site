# Catalog Card Direct Color Swatches Design

The smart catalog card keeps the approved Variant B composition unchanged. Its square color toggle is replaced by up to three direct circular variant links under the heart, using the homepage swatch language: a full color surface, fine light border, restrained depth, and a warm active ring.

There is no disclosure panel, count badge, color text, or new interaction layer. Each circle leads to the corresponding product variant URL. Split-color and thermochromic variants remain supported; the thermo flame is centered and black inside the swatch.

The group is right-aligned at the existing Variant B vertical position and sized to remain compact in two-column mobile cards. Discount position, heart, 4:5 image ratio, title, current and old prices, fit badges, header, footer, filters, and SEO content remain out of scope.
